FLUID — Scene Workspace

Add Actor > Simulation > Fluid creates a finite liquid volume. Properties contains
Fluid (Water/Oil/Honey/Slime/Lava presets), Quality, Source, Interactions, Appearance,
Texture and Surface sections. Actor > Physics controls activation, gravity, source
attachments, collision masks, freeze, cache and baking. Presets are one undo action;
ordinary parameters use existing property editing/keyframing. Source time follows
clip trimming/speed. Moving the source never teleports already emitted samples.

Solver: position-based fluids, Poly6 density, Spiky pressure gradients, XSPH
viscosity, short-range cohesion/tension and CFL-limited substeps. Forward+/Mobile
uses a local RenderingDevice compute pipeline, spatially hashed neighbor chains,
bounded neighbors and sorted-ID reductions for reproducible replay. There is no
live engine timestep. Compatibility/headless rendering uses the CPU implementation,
limited to 256 particles. A full initial volume over its budget is rejected, not
silently cut off. Closed mesh volumes validate paired triangle edges and use BVH
inside tests. Keep the source scale at one; change Dimensions for predictable
particle resolution. Mesh fill is the closed source mesh's local shape, placed at
the Fluid source transform, not a continuously deforming liquid boundary.

Collision: configure surrounding actors' Actor > Physics as Static collider or
Animated collider, or leave their dynamic body enabled. Contacts sweep against
the same inactive Jolt query world used by the other timeline physics. Fluid
boundary friction and impact response use collider surface velocity. Containers
can be assembled from thick boxes or closed static concave geometry. Very thin
walls and extreme speeds need finer spacing/substeps. Bone-origin sphere proxies
and soft/cloth/rope point proxies are optional approximations, not sealed clothing
or articulated capsule meshes. Response is ONE-WAY: no buoyancy, rigid reaction
force, inter-fluid mixing or two-way cloth coupling is claimed.

Rendering: sparse implicit density field reconstructed by marching tetrahedra into
one world-space ArrayMesh. No sphere particles or thousands of actor nodes. A
custom material slot accepts the ordinary Material, Natural Material or Shader
Material resources. Default shader supports color, opacity, roughness, metallic,
specular, emission, noise/image detail, world triplanar color mapping, normal map,
texture scale/offset/rotation and flow driven by timeline time (not shader TIME).
Impact foam follows collision intensity and fades; this is surface shading, not
a separate bubble solver. Appearance and surface changes do not invalidate the
particle cache. Physical source/quality/motion edits do. Foam duration is applied
on subsequent live steps. Freeze/bake stores IDs, positions, velocities, masses,
density ratios, foam, emission counters and source state. Emission is deterministic
and the budget/supply is finite. Seeking and video export wait for physics replay.

Performance is bounded, not a claim of production-scale real-time simulation.
Measurements: .godot/verification/fluid_benchmark.json, generated by
fluid_solver_test.gd on the actual renderer/CPU. The native GPU density solver is
substantially faster than the CPU fallback; synchronous readback/boundary queries
and CPU surface reconstruction remain costs. Hundreds of detailed particles can
render below real-time speed; bake for editing/rendering. The surface has a
180,000-grid-vertex safety budget and reports overflow rather than hanging. The
current reconstructed surface is a clean interface for a future stylized renderer;
no unimplemented M2DO integration, heat simulation or hidden external service is
advertised. Lava temperature alters viscosity and contact metadata, not other
actors' materials. fluid_contacts emits bounded impact records for future consumers.

Tests: fluid_solver_test (density/shear, closed/open volume validation, GPU and
performance), fluid_physics_test (collapse/spread, floor collision, pause/seek,
presets/undo, emission, bake and native scene persistence), fluid_workspace_test
(real Scene Workspace UI and two temporary scene-video renders). Existing rigid,
cloth, rope and skeleton regression suites are also run. Application export is
not part of these tests.
