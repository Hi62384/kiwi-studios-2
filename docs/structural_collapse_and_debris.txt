Structural collapse, physical debris and destruction effects
============================================================
Godot 4.6.1. This extends the existing timeline physics, offline collision world,
material-damage and convex fracture engines. No independent physics process,
engineering workspace, application export or ProjectBridge ZIP is introduced.

Build a structure
-----------------
1. Add Basic Shape / Basic Mesh actors, or import a model with separate components.
2. Properties > Structure: switch Enabled on and choose Dynamic
   element, Fixed foundation or Animated support. Ordinary Physics controls still
   supply mass, gravity, velocities, forces and timed impulses. The structural
   role supplies the rigid body mode while structural simulation is enabled.
3. Choose a target and joint type, then click +. Assemble selection enables a
   group of selected meshes without merging them or changing scene parenting.
4. Set breaking force (N), torque (N.m), strength multiplier and stiffness (N/m).
   Joint details contains directional resistance, damping, accumulated/initial
   damage, endpoint offsets, orientation and material-strength linking.
5. Find nearby reviews bounds-based candidates among selected meshes at the
   chosen tolerance. Clicking a candidate explicitly accepts it. Finding nearby
   objects, importing a hierarchy or touching another object NEVER welds it.
6. Edit joint in viewport / Joints J exposes selectable endpoint/direction
   handles. Drag endpoints to move their local pivot; drag direction to orient
   the joint. Ctrl snaps, Shift-wheel edits strength, Escape cancels/exits.
   Gold/blue identify the two endpoints. Incoming links can be opened from the
   target inspector. The normal transform tools remain available outside Joints.

Connections and load approximation
-----------------------------------
Weld: positional and orientation constraints.
Hinge: positional constraints, with rotation free about the configured Y axis.
Slider: transverse and orientation constraints, with bounded axial travel.
Cable/distance: radial constraint, optionally tension-only with slack.
Spring: compliant radial constraint. Set length and stiffness to tune stretch.

The solver predicts existing rigid motion, then iterates compliant XPBD rigid
joint constraints. Translational and rotational correction impulses estimate
reaction forces and moments. Geometry size/mass set approximate scalar inertia;
lever arms contribute to bending utilization. Gravity, forces, motion and
collisions therefore change measured joint loads rather than simply counting
connections. Strength, directional multipliers and accumulated damage determine
failure. Prevent failure protects stress failures; an explicit Break at event
still wins. Constraints cease when broken. Connected unanchored islands fall.

Island topology is rebuilt when nodes or intact links change. Contacts are a
separate directed compressive support relation, not permanent welds. A platform
may remain resting on a column after its weld breaks: that is intentional. The
overlay uses green-to-red estimated utilization, red broken links and support
markers. These are approximate artistic loads, NOT certified stress analysis.

Stress/torque work is fed into the existing local material-damage engine. Slender
members receive a bounded compression amplification. Material yield, toughness,
brittleness, hardness, directionality and advanced coefficients then determine
dent/crack/fracture outcomes. This is not a continuum buckling/FEM solver.
Escalating automatic physical damage may replace earlier automatic dents/cracks
with fracture of the original closed source, because the existing cutter cannot
fracture arbitrary concave deformed topology. Authored manual operation order is
unchanged. Local accumulated damage remains. Compatible surviving fragment
material retains its attachment; destroyed/missing attachment material releases.

Modeling integration
---------------------
The Model Workspace provides structural role and Connect selected components
controls. Connections/configuration save on each component. Model instantiation
preserves their stable IDs and relationships as individual scene mesh actors.
Scene and model assembly duplication remaps internal targets; scene copies clear
external targets rather than silently joining the original. Renaming is safe.
Missing targets produce warnings. World-space bakes of copies must be rebuilt.
The Scene Structure inspector is the detailed joint editor, not a second model
engineering workspace. Model hierarchy remains independent from mechanical links.

Directing and timeline behavior
-------------------------------
Diamond buttons keyframe strength, stiffness, enable/protection, endpoints,
orientation and numeric joint controls. Break at uses source time (-1 disables).
Support activation/role can be animated. Existing Physics force/impulse events
remain usable. Authored edits are undoable; simulation steps generate no undo
entries. Deleting a connection removes its tracks in the same undo action.
Checkpoints/bakes preserve geometry, fragments, joint integrity/damage, islands,
events and transforms. Pause does not advance. Rewind restores earlier support
and fracture states; seeking reconstructs fixed-step simulation. Appearance-only
surface/effect edits do not invalidate trajectories. Physical edits do.

Physical debris workflow
-------------------------
Properties > Destruction enables the existing operations/material damage.
Properties > Debris & Effects controls physical debris, collisions, budgets,
fracture impulse, secondary fracture, automatic effects and a custom VFX resource.
Advanced controls are collapsed. A zero impulse multiplier keeps only inherited
source translation/rotation. The authored operation's impulse is an explicit
artistic separation impulse, not a physically inferred explosion.

Real generated geometry is retained. Fragments have stable IDs, volume-weighted
mass, world transforms, linear/angular motion and convex collision hulls. Small
pieces can use sphere proxies. Their center and inertia use bounded approximations
(bounds center / scalar inertia), not exact mesh integrals. Material friction and
restitution supply contact defaults; disable that advanced option to use the
actor's Physics contact settings. Material density derives mass only when the
existing Use material density for mass option is enabled.

Fragments collide with each other and compatible rigid/animated actors, exchange
impulses and can feed secondary impact energy into the existing damage engine.
Energetic brittle chunks may fracture again. Minimum size, energy threshold,
secondary depth and count limits prevent runaway recursion. Source geometry and
original colliders are restored on rewind; the intact collider is disabled while
fragments are active. Settled fragments can sleep and wake on nearby motion.

Kernel-sampled fluid drag and approximate buoyancy exchange opposite momentum
with fluid samples. Real fragment hulls displace liquid via existing boundary
queries. Powder queries also see fragment hulls and exchange grain contact
impulses. These couplings are approximations, not resolved hydrodynamics or
continuous granular terrain. Full two-way cloth/soft-body pressure and a new
fragment-to-character skin collider are NOT supplied: existing deformable point
queries can hit debris, but debris does not solve a new deformable surface model.

Copy debris to editable actors creates an ordinary mesh-actor snapshot under a
point, in one undo action. Original simulation remains intact; copied actors have
physics disabled by default to avoid accidentally doubling simulated mass. This
is a copy, not transfer of ownership out of an active simulation inventory.

Automatic effects
------------------
Physical fracture, impact, scraping, secondary fracture and overload events carry
time, source/fragment identity, contact position/normal, relative velocity,
intensity and deterministic seed. Per-contact time deduplication and thresholds
prevent resting contacts producing bursts every step. Glass/ceramic makes chips;
metal makes energetic impact/scraping sparks (not cold-cut sparks); wood makes
splinters/sawdust; stone/concrete/dirt makes dust and chips. Current material and
artist overrides select the rendering rules. Custom Destruction VFX Preset objects
can replace or augment automatic rules and use ordinary save/load/Make Unique/
clipboard references. Materials can supply a default effect family/preset.

Physical fragment mass and visual particle detail are separate. Particle detail
does not add mass or a second set of physical grains. Godot particle draw meshes
and materials are rendered in pooled MultiMeshes. An analytic burst-age adapter
reconstructs positions/colors at scene time instead of free-running GPU simulation.
Changing playback/export FPS cannot change event times or random samples. Dust
is soft billboard particle shading, not a volumetric solver. No full smoke, heat,
combustion or material micro-fracture solver is implied; stylization uses custom
colors/sizes/motion, not a dedicated M2DO renderer.

Limits and practical performance
--------------------------------
Structure: up to 512 elements / 1,024 explicit joints; quality 2-32 iterations.
Debris: 256 physical pieces scene-wide, per-actor budget up to 256; existing convex
geometry operations retain their 48-piece / 20,000-source-vertex limits. More
complex cuts are refused clearly, preserving source geometry, rather than silently
dropping mass. Secondary fracture is limited to depth 0-4 and at most four children
per split. Events: 96 per actor, 512 recent-contact stamps, up to 8,192 visible VFX
particles. Existing checkpoint/bake budgets include estimates for debris/events.
Each important physical piece has an internal mesh node, not an ordinary actor;
visual microdetail is batched. Geometry preparations and collision exclusions are
cached. Large structures are CPU-heavy and should be baked, not promised 60 FPS.

No arbitrary concave/rigged topology cutting, exact inertia tensor, arbitrary
joint angular limits/motors, face-painted structural bonds, continuum FEM, cable
collision, or realistic heterogeneous welded-solid fracture is added. Different
playback-speed actors in one constrained assembly are not a calibrated physical
unit system; prefer a shared playback rate. Discrete fixed-step queries/approximate
rotation can still miss extreme-speed/thin contacts; raise quality/use smaller
time steps or bake suitable artistic motion. Candidate detection is conservative
world-AABB proximity, not automatic precise surface welding.
There is no merged-rigid-cluster solver or complete sleeping-island optimization:
intact joint networks continue fixed-step constraint evaluation. Island detection
is cached while topology is unchanged, but a topology change rebuilds the small
assembly graph rather than incrementally repartitioning only affected subgraphs.

Verification records
----------------------
Native tests, regression reports and screenshots live in .godot/verification/.
The delivery report lists the actual final passing runs and measured performance.
Intermediate integration/test-fixture failures are retained in earlier logs;
they are not presented as passing results. Root-certificate-store and some
existing broad-test shutdown resource warnings may remain. No application was
exported; short scene-video validation clips are temporary and removed afterwards.

Measured on this machine, 2026-10-26 (not a real-time FPS guarantee):
- Isolated joint solve, 12 iterations: 16 elements / 15 joints about 5.7 ms;
  64 / 63 about 36.1 ms; 128 / 127 about 78.9 ms per step. Stable topology needed
  one island rebuild across the benchmark. Full scene/collision costs are extra.
- A small secondary-impact debris scenario measured about 2.9 ms for its last
  debris step while 1,006 visual particles were displayed. This is not a
  worst-case 256-fragment performance claim.
- The two-floor test carried approximately 55.6 N on each foundation connection.
  After one directed support break, the other exceeded its 80 N limit (about
  94 N at failure), releasing both connected floors. Independently supported
  sections and loose compressive contacts are covered by separate tests.

Native collision filtering was also repaired: the completed excluded-RID list
must be assigned to Godot, not appended to the temporary returned by its getter.
This prevents overlapping welded components creating false collision overloads.
Powder intrusion queries independently inspect real contact penetration when a
native overlap reports zero travel, so excluding the supporting floor does not
hide a wheel's penetration into attached grains.
