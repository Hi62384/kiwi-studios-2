Volumetric lighting implementation / evidence — 2026-10-27

Native integration
- Existing Directional, Omni and Spot actors light native FogVolume data. General
  Light now exposes volumetric energy, source size, distance/cone attenuation and
  native shadow bias, normal bias, opacity and softness. Dedicated Directional
  controls retain their previous properties. Zero volumetric energy leaves ordinary
  surface Energy unchanged; there are no duplicate fog-only light actors/passes.
- Basic Mesh/Shape, Text 3D and Image 3D actors expose shadow casting: Keep original,
  Off, On, Double-sided, Shadows only. Child actors retain their own settings.
  Render-only shadow controls do not invalidate physics signatures or portable bakes.
- World Environment exposes temporal reprojection, history strength and ambient
  injection alongside its existing anisotropy, GI, sky, distance and detail settings.
  Native Environment setters do not always emit changed, so the temporary fog
  environment detects actual property changes using a cached property layout.
- Editor opens project-wide volumetric sampling: Fast 64/32, Balanced 128/64,
  Detailed 192/96, Final 256/128, custom bounded dimensions, filtering and scene
  positional shadow atlas 1024/2048/4096/8192. Sampling is renderer-wide; shadow
  atlas is the scene viewport's and is copied to camera previews. Settings persist
  in Project.fog_rendering and are undoable through the scene model history.

Shadows and shafts
- Real shadow maps and opaque scene depth determine shadowed fog and light shafts.
  Window openings, moving walls and colored overlapping lights are native geometry /
  light interactions, not transparent cone or dark overlay geometry.
- Reproduced stale shadow-atlas binding when shadows become enabled after fog data
  exists. On a changed shadow-light topology / atlas configuration, the coordinator
  changes internal render scale for one frame to recreate native buffers and restores
  the exact authored scale. It does not do this for moving light/caster transforms.
  Confirmed by native pixel tests: behind-wall intensity 0.28009 -> 0.20232 with
  shadows enabled; moving the caster restores light. Upstream context:
  https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/83685
- Godot also has an inherent spot volumetric-shadow fade limitation at distance.
  The integration does not claim to remove the engine's hardcoded fade or provide
  independent per-volume light linking / anisotropy. Upstream discussion:
  https://github.com/godotengine/godot/issues/80039
- Exclusion is explicitly authored with primitive, painted or closed-mesh fields.
  It changes allowed density, rather than pretending a solid wall automatically
  causes airtight flow. Mesh occupancy is cached, rigid and bounded at 32³. Full
  deforming-mesh SDFs and automatically actor-following exclusion links are not
  implemented. Source meshes remain intact.

Timeline / scene video
- Light transforms/colors/energy/volumetric energy/angle and caster transforms use
  normal actor tracks, property gestures, undo and scene serialization.
- Scene-frame capture marks its viewport, explicitly synchronizes fog ownership
  after actor/camera evaluation, disables fog temporal history in temporary world /
  camera overrides and waits two render completions before reading each exact time.
  Particle/physics time stays fixed during warm-up. Original history settings and
  capture flags are restored; source Environment resources are never rewritten.
- fog_export_test captures actual red / purple / blue fog on all three intended
  frames. Previously frame zero was gray and later colors were history-blended;
  that failure was fixed, not hidden by changing the color assertions.
- fog_lighting_export_test encodes actual animated light / moving-caster scenes at
  24 and 48 fps. Six matching center-pixel samples were identical in the measured
  fixture. This is not a claim of universal bit-identical rendering. Temporary
  MP4s are removed; no application export was performed.

Verified native Forward+ reports
- fog_lighting_regressions: all 11 broader actor/scene/shader/growth/bake/clipboard
  scripts pass after the lighting and export changes.
- lighting_quality_final: four scripts pass (settings, spotlight/wall shadow,
  window shafts and fog scene video). Later fog_multi_light_depth expands lighting
  rendering to 11 assertions, all passing.
- fog_light_video_retry: animated lighting/caster MP4 tests, 4 assertions passing.
- fog_exclusion_pixels: primitive, closed-mesh and painted-mask pixels, 3 passing.
- fog_environment_live: live anisotropy/window shafts and appearance rendering pass;
  its settings fixture initially placed the environment clip at 5s while testing
  0.5s. The fixture now uses an explicit free layer/start and all 8 controls/save/
  undo/keyframe assertions pass in fog_light_controls_final / lighting_quality_final.
- fog_native_shadow_fix: real shadow/moving caster test and fog video pass.
- Failed intermediate reports are retained, not counted as successful final checks.

Performance actually obtained (simple 512x384 native RTX 3060 Laptop fixture)
- One volumetric light, no shadows, one volume, 64/32 froxels: viewport render CPU
  0.151–0.217 ms, GPU 0.216–0.311 ms.
- Four lights, no shadows, one volume, 64/32: CPU 0.154–0.300 ms, GPU 0.335–0.348 ms.
- Four shadow lights, one volume, 64/32: CPU 0.175–0.343 ms, GPU 0.622–0.678 ms.
- Four shadow lights, three volumes, 64/32: CPU 0.176–0.307 ms, GPU 0.668–0.698 ms.
- Four shadow lights, three volumes, 192/96: CPU 0.135–0.266 ms; GPU steady
  1.922–1.931 ms, with initial transition samples 7.638–11.466 ms.
- Later process-frame intervals mostly 16.1–17.1 ms under vsync. First partial-frame
  intervals are not full frame measurements. Viewport renderer timings exclude
  the complete editor, script simulation and production-scene costs. These fixtures
  use native simple fog materials, not a worst-case five-influence appearance stack.

Limits
- Native single scattering, not multiple scattering/path tracing. Fog emission
  does not illuminate scene meshes; use an actual light for that.
- Alpha/transparency/custom-shader shadow/depth behavior follows Godot. No claimed
  physically correct colored transparent shadows or custom transparency solution.
- Resolution and distance affect thin shafts, banding and cost. High temporal
  history can trail in interactive playback; it is editable and disabled for exact
  frame capture. Full skinned-character shadow and large production-asset visual
  coverage remains broader than the current primitive/proxy acceptance fixtures.
