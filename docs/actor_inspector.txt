ACTOR INSPECTOR
===============
The two main tabs at the top stay fixed while property fields scroll:

Actor: shared settings such as the actor's name, position, rotation, scale and
screen-space layout/size. Transform is the initial Actor section.

Properties: settings specific to the selected actor type, including shapes,
media, materials, camera settings, rig poses and transformation-image states.

Sections are tabs down the left side. Only one section is shown at a time.
Long section lists scroll separately, and the selected section is brought into
view when restored. Long tab names have full-name tooltips and the active page
also shows its full section heading. Nested resources retain their expandable
controls within the relevant page.

Each actor remembers its main tab, section within each main tab, and scroll
position within each section. This state is retained across reselection,
inspector rebuilds, scene tabs and native scene save/load. The existing nested
resource expansion and vector-link settings remain independent. Navigation is
editor state, not an undo action; property changes and keyframes still use the
normal scene undo/redo history.

File previews temporarily replace the actor inspector and hide its actor tabs.
Selecting an actor restores that actor's inspector navigation.

ActorProperty options may explicitly set inspector_tab to Actor or Properties.
Otherwise position, rotation, scale and the Transform/Layout groups are shared
Actor settings; all other definition properties are type-specific Properties.
